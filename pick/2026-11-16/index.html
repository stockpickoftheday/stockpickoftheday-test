Amazon, in plain English

A 2-minute read
01 / THE BUSINESS

What does Amazon actually do?

Amazon sells products online and helps other sellers reach shoppers. It also earns money from advertising and Amazon Web Services, which rents computing power to businesses.

02 / THE DAILY LESSON

Why this company today?

Amazon is a helpful example of a company with several different businesses. The part you use most is not necessarily the part that makes the most profit.

KEEP IN MIND

Delivery operations are costly. Cloud competitors and rules about online marketplaces may affect growth and profits.

THE SIMPLE TAKEAWAY
Look at the whole business, not only the product you use. Different parts can earn very different amounts of profit.
